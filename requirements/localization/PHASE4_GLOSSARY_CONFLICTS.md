# PHASE4_GLOSSARY_CONFLICTS

| Term | Wrong ZH | Canonical | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (I) | 当前 | **电流** | Physics quantity only in this batch |
| Current (UI “current value”) | 电流 | **当前** / avoid | Not used as I-label in Phase 4 bags |
| Charge | 充电 | **电荷** | Noun = electric charge |
| Power | 电源 | **功率** | Unifyary with mechanics `mechanics.power` |
| Cell | 单元格 | **电池** | Battery cell in circuit sims |
| Potential | 潜力 | **电势** / **电势差** | Electric potential |
| Field | 场 (ambiguous) | **电场** / **磁场** | Always qualify |
| Resistance vs Resistor | 电阻混用 | R=**电阻**; component=**电阻器** | CCK component palette |
| Capacitance vs Capacitor | 电容混用 | C=**电容**; component=**电容器** | CLB + CCK |
| Conventional | 传统 | **常规电流** | Current-direction radio |

Updates applied to `GLOSSARY_CONFLICTS.md` / `LOCALIZATION_GLOSSARY.md` where needed.
